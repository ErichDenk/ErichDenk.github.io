Erich Denk

Public Policy • Economics • Research

I am a policy researcher and data analyst with interest in the future of work, entrepreneurship, and labor markets. I also have interests in data science, R programming, and statistical analysis. I combine rigorous economic analysis with practical policy insights communicated in a clear and digestible way.

I hold a Master of Public Policy from Georgetown University, where I developed a foundation in quantitative analysis and evidence-based policymaking. I also serve as an instructional associate in courses such as Program Evaluation and Introduction to Microeconomics.

This page serves as a hub for my writing, research, and assorted projects.

Currently, I am a data analyst at Mathematica, though views on this page are my own and do not reflect those of my employer.
